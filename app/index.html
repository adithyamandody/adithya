It is a yes / no game

The computer asks “is your letter in this group?” and halves the group each time until one letter is left.

  1. A group lights up teal. That is the question.
  2. Your letter in it? Press space or tap the screen — that means yes.
  3. Not in it? Do nothing. Waiting means no, and the bar running out answers for you.

Doing nothing is a real answer. That is the part that feels strange — half the time the right move is to sit still.

Two switches give a third command. A long hold works on its own, because pressing two switches at once is hard with impaired motor control — and a chord works too, because some people find that easier than holding.

Voice

The device voice works offline and is the fallback for everything. A cloud voice sounds better but needs a network — so every clip is cached after the first fetch and plays offline from then on.

⚠️ Keys are stored on this device and are visible to anyone who can open its browser tools. Use a key restricted to text-to-speech with a spending cap, and never a key that can do anything else.

Switch test
